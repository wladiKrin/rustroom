> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans.

**Goal:** Lightroom-style purple/green defringe, global (Lens group) and per mask (−100..+100),
one prefix step after geometry, with an eyedropper. Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-m9.6-defringe-design.md`.

**Gate:** fmt · `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=target/clippy cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` · `cargo test --workspace`.

### Task 1 — `edit-model`
- [ ] `Defringe` (+ `is_active`, defaults), `Edits.defringe` in the Lens group, `merge_from`,
  fixtures; `LocalAdjustments.defringe`. Tests: old sidecar → neutral; `is_active` on amounts only.

### Task 2 — Mask coverage as a method

  used (a +1 EV mask's lift / expected lift).
