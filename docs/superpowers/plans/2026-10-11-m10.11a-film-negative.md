# M10.11a — film negative inversion: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use `- [ ]`.
> **Commits carry no `Co-Authored-By` trailer. Never push.**

**Goal:** A scanned negative becomes a scene-linear positive in one eyedropper click on the film base,
balanced per channel from the frame, stored in the edits; every later tool works forwards on it.

**Architecture:** `edit_model::Negative` (its own group, off by default) → `pipeline` prefix step
`NegativeStage` right after lens correction, with the per-pixel `positive` exported → app lib
`negative_tool` (pick, measure) and every reader of the decode routed through `positive` → bin Negative
block, eyedropper, thumbnails of RAW negatives from the raw decode.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, `edit-model`, `pipeline` (prefix steps, golden harness), egui.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-m10.11a-film-negative-design.md`.

## Global Constraints

- The stage math: `d = log10(base / max(px, 1e-6))`, `e(d) = 10^(d/γ)`, `out = max(0, (e(d) − e(low)) /
  (e(high) − e(low))) · 0.9`; Black & white averages `px / base` before the log and uses the mean low/high.
- Defaults: off, γ 0.6, `low` 0, `high` 2.0 per channel, base `[0; 3]` (unpicked = inactive), mono off.
- The Negative group is the 11th, **appended** (`NEGATIVE_GROUP = 10`), **off by default**; not reset by
  Reset adjustments.
- Percentiles 0.5 / 99.5 on a 512 px analysis inside the crop.
- No egui in `edit-model`, `pipeline` or app lib modules. No new crates.
- Hover text: one short sentence, no full stop. Gate unpiped; commit on `rc == 0`; sabotage only a
  committed tree.

## Review Focus

1. **A frame with no visible film base** (cropped tight, a full-frame scan) — the pick can be made on any
   frame of the roll and synced; picking a dark area by mistake gives `high ≤ low` somewhere and the stage
   stays inactive rather than producing garbage. (Task 2 test.)
2. **Sprocket holes or a bright scanner border inside the crop** — `px > base` gives a negative density,
   which levels to black, never NaN. (Task 2 test.)
3. **A negative synced to a roll whose frames differ in exposure** — the measured densities belong to the
   picked frame; *Auto* on each frame re-measures from its own pixels. (Task 3 test.)
4. **Undo after a pick** — base, on and both densities return in one step. (Task 4 test.)
5. **A JPEG/TIFF scan** — the same stage on the decoded file, and its thumbnail from its own pixels.
   (Task 5 test.)
