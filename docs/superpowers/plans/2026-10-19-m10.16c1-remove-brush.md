# M10.16c1 — the Remove brush — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Retouch's **Remove** mode: paint over an object, and a local inpainting network fills it from its surroundings — with Refresh, Detect objects and Opacity — cached, rendered as a prefix step after AI denoise, and exported.

**Architecture:** A removal is an edit (`Edits.removals`) holding its stroke in normalised decode space. Its **fill** — a patch (a decode-space rectangle, its RGB and soft mask) — is computed once in the background from the full-resolution decode by the network (`ai_mask::inpaint`), optionally after MobileSAM grows the stroke to the object (Detect objects), cached on disk, and handed to the pipeline through `AiRasters` like AI denoise's result. A prefix step right after AI denoise pastes each fill at the frame's size at its Opacity.

**Tech Stack:** Rust, `tract` (ai-mask), `rayon`, `egui` 0.35.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-m10.16c-remove-design.md` (§ c1; c2–c4 have their own plans).

## Global Constraints

- Local only; nothing leaves the computer. Downloads only through the model-download offer, and the spike's with the author's OK (file, source, size named).
- The candidates: **LaMa** `Carve/LaMa-ONNX` `lama_fp32.onnx`, Apache-2.0, 208,044,816 bytes, 512 × 512; **MI-GAN** `andraniksargsyan/migan` `migan_pipeline_v2.onnx`, MIT, 28,079,181 bytes. The author picks from the spike's sheets.
- Retouch's modes: **Remove · Heal · Clone · Red eye · Pet eye**, Remove first and the default.
- The prefix order: **AI denoise → removals → lens …** (a removal after the denoise step, or the object comes back).
- A new `Edits` field is `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "…")]`, joins its `EditGroups` group (Retouch, with `spots` and `eyes`), `merge_from`'s destructure and the exhaustive fixtures.
- Every reader of the undeveloped decode sees the positive (`negative_tool`/`pipeline::negative::positive`); no AI request from a ghost session.
- A prefix step reading derived data keys on its CONTENT id.
- DESIGN.md: hovers one sentence, no full stop, ≤ 110 chars; no literal font size or `Color32` outside `theme.rs`.
- Repo rules: never push; no `Co-Authored-By`; one cargo mode; no file over 1,000 lines of code; placement per `docs/PARALLEL-WORK.md` §5 (logic in the lib, egui in the bin).

## Review Focus

1. **A stroke at the photo's edge** — the context window clamps to the decode; the fill still lands where painted. Pinned in Task 4 (`a_window_at_the_edge_stays_inside`).
2. **AI denoise on, then a removal** — the object must not come back through the denoised result. Pinned in Task 3 (`a_removal_after_ai_denoise_stays_removed`).
3. **Refresh** — a new `variant` gives a different fill, never the cached one. Pinned in Task 4 (`refresh_moves_the_window_and_the_key`).
4. **A film negative** — the fill is computed on the positive. Pinned in Task 5 (`the_fill_reads_the_positive`).
5. **Export of a photo whose fill was never computed** — computed there and then, never exported with the object. Pinned in Task 5 (`an_export_computes_a_missing_fill`).
