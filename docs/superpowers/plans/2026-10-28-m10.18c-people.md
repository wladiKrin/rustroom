# M10.18c People: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** A People list in the rail and a People view in the Library.
- Immich's people are read in. An Immich person opens as a server-backed source.
- Rustroom finds faces in local photos with YuNet and SFace, groups look-alikes, and lets the author name a group
  at once.
- The top row's views can be hidden.

**Architecture:**
- `ai-mask::faces` holds the two networks.
- Catalog v16 holds faces, people, rejections, and which photos were read.
- Lib modules handle grouping (`face_groups`), the background search (`face_pass`) and the views setting.
- Bin modules: `people_view` (the view), `face_ops` (naming plus undo), the rail section, and the Immich people
  source.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-m10.18c-people-design.md`

**Spike, done 2026-10-28:** both networks run in tract (`crates/ai-mask/examples/probe_faces.rs`).
- **YuNet 2023mar** (MIT, 232,589 B, sha `8f2383e4…52fa4`) at 1×3×640×640 takes 88 ms. It returns 12 outputs:
  cls/obj `[1,N,1]`, bbox `[1,N,4]` and kps `[1,N,10]` at strides 8/16/32 (N = 6400/1600/400).
- **SFace 2021dec** (Apache-2.0, 38,696,353 B, sha `0ba9fbfa…34e79`) at 1×3×112×112 takes 49 ms and returns
  `[1,128]`.

## Global constraints

- **Names, faces and embeddings stay in the catalog.** Never a file, a sidecar or Immich. Immich is read, never
  written.
- No InsightFace weights.
- **Detection input:** a RAW's embedded JPEG at its own size, or another file's decode at 2,048 px long. Never the
  512-px thumbnail.
- **Nothing per frame grows with the catalog:**
  - the rail goes through `RailData`;
  - groups are memoised on `(instance, change_count, visible_rev)`;
  - the view lays out only what is visible.
- Hover text is one sentence with no full stop. An icon must be in the cmap: `users` is U+E4D6, checked against the
  2.1.2 stylesheet and `fc-query`.
- The gate: `cargo test --workspace` (never `-p`), clippy in `target/clippy`, and `cargo fmt --check`.
- No file over 1,000 lines of code and doc. Test directories come from `test_dir`. Waits on an event have a 120 s
  ceiling.

## Review focus

1. A named or confirmed face is never moved by a later grouping. Test: `grouping_never_moves_a_named_face`.
2. "No" is final: a rejected group is never offered as that person again, even after more faces arrive. Test:
   `a_rejected_person_is_not_offered_again`.
3. An Immich-only photo is never searched locally, and an undownloaded one gets no `faces_read` row. Test:
   `the_face_pass_skips_immich_and_absent_files`.
4. A move carries faces, and a forget/restore brings them back. Tests: `a_moved_photo_keeps_its_faces`,
   `forgetting_a_folder_and_undoing_brings_back_its_faces`.
5. Hiding the open view returns to Grid, and its shortcut still opens it. Test:
   `hiding_the_open_view_returns_to_grid`.

### Task 1: `ai-mask::faces`

**What:**
- Registry entries `YunetFace` and `SfaceFace`.
  - URLs: `https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/raw/main/models/face_detection_yunet/face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx`
    and `…/face_recognition_sface/face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx`.
  - The 1 MB floor gets a named exception for YuNet.
- `pub struct Face { pub bbox: [f32; 4] /* x0,y0,x1,y1 normalised */, pub landmarks: [[f32; 2]; 5] /* normalised */, pub score: f32 }`
- `pub struct Faces`:
  - `load(yunet: &Path, sface: &Path) -> Result<Self, InferError>`
  - `detect(&self, img: &RgbImage) -> Result<Vec<Face>, InferError>`: letterbox to 640, BGR 0..255, decode the
    three strides (score = √(cls·obj), centre = (grid + Δ)·stride, size = exp(Δwh)·stride, kps =
    (grid + Δ)·stride), threshold 0.6, NMS 0.3.
  - `embed(&self, img: &RgbImage, f: &Face) -> Result<[f32; 128], InferError>`: a similarity transform of the five
    landmarks onto ArcFace's 112×112 template, then SFace, L2-normalised.
- `pub fn similarity(a: &[f32;128], b: &[f32;128]) -> f32`

**Tests:**
- `the_registry_pins_the_face_models`
- `decode_reads_one_constructed_face`: hand-made output tensors with one strong cell decode to its box.
- `the_alignment_maps_the_template_to_itself`
- `similarity_of_a_vector_with_itself_is_one`
- `#[ignore]` `a_portrait_has_one_face_and_two_crops_agree`: runs the real models on a committed public-domain
  portrait fixture.

### Task 3: Lib `face_groups`

**What:**
- `pub struct Group { pub faces: Vec<i64>, pub suggest: Option<i64 /*person*/> }`
- `pub fn group(unnamed: &[(i64, [f32;128])], named: &[(i64 /*person*/, [f32;128])], rejected: &HashSet<(i64,i64)>, threshold: f32) -> Vec<Group>`:
  - average-link agglomeration;
  - a suggestion when the group's mean is within the threshold of a person's confirmed faces' mean;
  - a person the group's faces rejected is never suggested;
  - largest groups first.
- `GroupMemo` is keyed on a `u64`.
- `pub const THRESHOLD: f32 = 0.363`

**Tests:**

### Task 8: The People view, its search and naming

**What:**
- `people_view.rs` (bin):
  - The named row shows face crops taken from the grid thumbnail, and from the embedded preview when the face is
    under 24 px.
  - The groups grid is virtual.
  - The name field submits on Enter. Yes/No answer a suggestion.
  - Clicking a face shows its photo with every face's box in a pane over the view. There a local face can be
    renamed, cleared or deleted.
- `face_ops.rs`: `name_group`, `confirm`, `reject`, `clear_face`, `delete_face`, each one
  `ActionKind::FaceChange` step.
- The pump starts a `FacePass` over the view's local, unread, on-disk photos when the view is open and
  `find_faces` is on.
- Missing models go through `Modal::AiDownload`.
- Status: "Looking for faces · 41 of 64 photos", with Cancel.
