# M9.10 — DNG export: the finished picture as a linear DNG

**Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Branch:** `m9.10-dng-export` · **Status:** choices made in brainstorm

Plan done-when: *a DNG opens in Lightroom and in darktable with the rendered image intact.*

## Decisions (the author's)

- **Option A — the finished picture**: every edit baked in, delivered as a raw-like linear DNG
  (not a cleaned-up raw, not a mosaic conversion).
- **Compressed**: lossless JPEG, as DNG readers expect for linear data.
- **No Lightroom here**: the done-when is judged in **darktable** (and RawTherapee, and libraw),
  by measurement against Rustroom's own 16-bit TIFF export. ⚠ darktable's default scene-referred
  workflow adds +0.7 EV and filmic/sigmoid to anything raw and ignores the DNG's profile tone curve;
  that will be measured and reported, with the setting that avoids it, not claimed away.

## The file (`export::dng`, `export::lj92`)

- IFD0: an 8-bit RGB preview (≤256 px), `Make` "Rustroom", `Model` "Rendered", and the DNG tags:
  `DNGVersion` 1.4, `UniqueCameraModel` "Rustroom Rendered" (deliberately NOT the source camera — a
  reader would apply that camera's profile to data that is no longer its colour), `ColorMatrix1` and
  `ForwardMatrix1` for linear ProPhoto at D50 (`CalibrationIlluminant1` = D50), `AsShotNeutral`
  1,1,1, `BaselineExposure` 0, `ProfileName` "Rustroom", an identity `ProfileToneCurve`,
  `ProfileEmbedPolicy` "no restrictions", `DefaultBlackRender` "none".
- SubIFD: the image — `LinearRaw`, 3 × 16-bit, `BlackLevel` 0, `WhiteLevel` 65535, 256×256 tiles,
  compression 7 (lossless JPEG, ITU T.81 process 14, predictor 1, one optimal Huffman table per
  tile). Orientation 1: geometry is already applied.
- Data: the render in **linear ProPhoto**, display-referred (the pipeline renders into ProPhoto and
  its encoding is undone), quantised to 16 bits.
- XMP (title, keywords, capture facts) through the existing TIFF post-pass.

## Export

`ExportFormat::Dng` — "DNG (linear, rendered)", extension `dng`. Colour space and JPEG quality
disabled for it in the dialog, with a hint; resize, output sharpening, watermark and templates as
for TIFF.

## Verification

Automated: the LJ92 encoder round-trips bit-exactly through a decoder written for the test; a DNG
decodes back through **libraw** to the source within a measured tolerance; the essential tags are
present. Measured and reported: darktable-cli (default workflow, and workflow "none") and
RawTherapee renders against the 16-bit TIFF export — per-pixel difference, and crops.
