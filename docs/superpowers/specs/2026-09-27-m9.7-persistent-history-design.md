# M9.7 — Persistent history

**Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Branch:** `m9.7-persistent-history` · **Status:** choices made in brainstorm

## What and why

Each photo's undo stack (`History<Edits>` on `Session`, parked in `HistoryStash` across photo
switches) dies at exit; reopening a photo the next day, Ctrl+Z does nothing. Plan done-when:
*reopening a photo offers the history it had at close, and the decision about where it lives
(sidecar versus catalog) is written down with its reason.*

## Decisions (the author's)

- **Ctrl+Z survives a restart; no new UI.** A History list was discussed and deferred: its unique
  value (jump back many steps, see what was done) is partly covered by snapshots, Before/After and
  the Now row; it can be built later on top of the stored data.
- **Only a photo's edit steps persist.** Renames, deleted copies/snapshots/catalog rows and the
  preset trash stay session-only — one-off actions, not a photo's editing history.
- ⚠⚠ **Stored in the CATALOG, not the sidecar — the decision the plan asked to be written down.**
  - The sidecar is the photograph's *description*: other tools, backups and the author read it,
    and it is rewritten on every save. History is *working state*. Measured sidecars are 3.4 KB
    median and 44 KB at most (DSCF7218, brush masks); each undo step is a whole `Edits`, so 100
    steps would make them 340 KB–4 MB.
  - The cost, accepted: a folder moved or renamed outside Rustroom, or a deleted catalog, loses
    the history — never the edits. Lightroom makes the same choice.

## Design

- **Catalog (migration 9):** `photo_history(folder_id, file_name, copy, body TEXT)`, primary key
  `(folder_id, file_name, copy)` — the `photos` key, so a virtual copy has its own; `ON DELETE
  CASCADE` from `folders`. `save_history`, `load_history`, `delete_history`, keyed by
  `(path, copy)`. The body is opaque text: the catalog stays free of `edit-model`.
- **`history_store` (lib, no egui):** `History` gains `into_parts` / `from_parts`; a serde document
  `{undo, current, redo}`; `encode(&History, cap)` keeps the **newest 100** undo steps;
  `decode(text) -> Option<History>`.
- **When:** saved wherever a photo's history is stashed (the one `finalize_session` choke point) and
  for the open photo at exit (`eframe::App::on_exit`); a history with nothing to undo or redo
  deletes its row. Restored in `finalize_session` when the in-memory stash misses: loaded, passed
  through the stash's existing rule — its `current` must equal the edits on disk, or it is
  discarded — and the `ActionLog` is **seeded** with its undo steps (in force) and redo steps
  (undone) as the OLDEST entries, so Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z reach them.
- **No catalog** (failed to open): exactly today's behaviour.
- ⚠ The snapshot strip's Now row counts changes since `History::initial()`; after a restore that is
  the oldest stored step, not the moment of opening — "since the history began".

## Tests

Catalog round-trip, delete, per-copy separation, cascade; encode/decode round-trip and the cap
keeping the newest; a stale history discarded; the log seeding placing restored steps below this
session's; **the whole cycle** — edit, drop the app, a new app on the same catalog, reopen, Ctrl+Z
walks back through the old edits in order.
