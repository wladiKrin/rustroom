# M9.8 — Keyword renaming and re-parenting

**Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Branch:** `m9.8-keyword-editing` · **Status:** choices made in brainstorm

Plan done-when: *renaming a keyword updates every file carrying it and its children, and the
operation appears in the action log and is undoable.*

## Decisions (the author's)

- **Rename in place** (double-click the name, the snapshot strip's field) and **drag to move**: onto
  another keyword nests it there; onto a **"Top level"** row — shown only while a keyword is being
  dragged — makes it a root. (The spec said "the Keywords header"; the section helper does not
  expose the header's response, and a drop row that appears with the drag is also easier to find.)
- **A name collision merges, after asking:** "Merge “Pariss” into “Paris”? 12 photos." Every photo
  carrying either keeps the survivor; sub-keywords merge the same way, recursively.

## Catalog — `db/keywords.rs` (a child module; `db.rs` is the crate's largest file)

- `rename_keyword(id, name) -> Change` and `move_keyword(id, parent) -> Change`, where
  `Change::{Done, Collides(existing_id)}`. A move under itself or a descendant is refused
  (`CatalogError`).
- `merge_keyword(from, into) -> KeywordMerge`: re-parent `from`'s children under `into` (a colliding
  child merges recursively), give `into` every file `from` had, delete `from`. The record lists
  exactly what changed — assignments added, children moved, rows deleted with their assignments —
  and `unmerge_keyword(&record)` reverses it, re-inserting deleted rows **with their original ids**
  so other undo entries naming them stay valid.
- `keyword_files(id)`: the distinct files carrying the keyword or any descendant — the XMP set.

## Undo — `ActionKind`

`KeywordRename {id, from, to}`, `KeywordMove {id, from, to}`, `KeywordMerge(Box<record>)`; global,
like every catalog action. Redo of a merge merges again.

## XMP

Every rename, move, merge, undo and redo rewrites the files of the affected subtree(s), collected
before the change, through `write_xmp_for` (the Settings toggle and the RAW-only rule). ⚠ **Also
fixed:** deleting a keyword (and undoing the delete) never rewrote XMP, so a deleted keyword stayed
in every RAW's sidecar.

## Lib rules — `keyword_tool`

`valid_name` (trimmed, non-empty, no `|` — the XMP hierarchy separator) and `can_drop(tree,
dragged, target)` (not onto itself, a descendant, or its current parent).

## Tests

Catalog: rename, move, the loop refusal, collisions reported, merge with a recursive child collision,
merge → unmerge restoring the exact rows and ids. Log round-trips. XMP: after a rename a tagged RAW's
`.xmp` carries the new path; after a delete it no longer carries the keyword. `keyword_tool` rules.
App: rename through the field; a drag re-parents; a colliding rename opens the merge confirm.
