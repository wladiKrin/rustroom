# M10.8b — Movable panels and a vertical filmstrip

**Date:** 2026-10-01 · **Branch:** `m10.8b-layout` · **Status:** design approved

Phase 10's M10.8b, *"a vertical filmstrip (2026-08-31)"*, widened by the author when the brainstorm
opened: *"all sidecars, i.e. the edit sidecar and also the one in the library view should be
movable"* — the side panels.

## Decisions (the author's)

- **The View menu, remembered** — Capture One's way; nothing moves by accident. Dragging a panel to the
  other side was offered and not chosen.
- **Each piece on its own:** the Library rail left or right; Develop's edit panel left or right (its
  tool rail stays on its outer edge); the filmstrip at the bottom, left or right.
- **A filmstrip on the edit panel's side sits at the window edge**, outside the tool rail, so the sliders
  stay beside the photo.

## Design

### The layout (lib `layout`, no egui)

`Side { Left, Right }`, `StripPlace { Bottom, Left, Right }` and `Layout { library_rail, edit_panel,
filmstrip }`, default **left / right / bottom** — today's — and a field of `Settings`, whose
container-level `#[serde(default)]` gives an older settings file today's layout. `develop_order(&Layout)`
is the order Develop's pieces are allocated in, **outermost first** (egui gives a side panel the room
left by those before it): `[Filmstrip, ToolRail, EditPanel]` when the strip shares the panel's side,
else `[ToolRail, EditPanel, Filmstrip]` — a bottom strip drawn after the side panels spans only the
photo column, as now. The status rows still follow, spanning the photo column.

### The menu

> ⚠ **Superseded after use (2026-10-01):** the author moved the three choices to **Settings → Appearance**
> (a *Layout* group of segmented controls); the View menu keeps only the *Filmstrip* toggle, with its
> check mark. The text below is the first build.

View, under *Hide panels*:

```
  ✓ Filmstrip
  ─────────────────────────
    Library rail on the right
    Edit panel on the left
  ─────────────────────────
  ✓ Filmstrip at the bottom
    Filmstrip on the left
    Filmstrip on the right
```

`Command::ToggleLibraryRailSide`, `ToggleEditPanelSide` and `FilmstripAt(StripPlace)`; choosing a place
also shows the strip. **Check marks are new**, and come from the command, so `Item` keeps its shape:
`menu::checked(command, &MenuState) -> Option<bool>` answers for the toggles and choices — the three
above and the existing *Filmstrip* — and `None` for an ordinary action. The in-window bar draws a
checkbox or a radio for those; macOS uses muda's `CheckMenuItem`, set from the state by a per-frame
`native_menu::sync` that touches AppKit only when the state changed.

### The pieces

- **Library rail:** `collections_panel` takes its side from the layout.
- **Edit panel and tool rail:** `develop::body` draws `develop_order`; the panel and the rail take the
  edit panel's side.
- **Filmstrip:** one code path along either axis. At the bottom, unchanged. On a side, a resizable side
  panel (default 150 wide, at least 96) holding one column; the cells are as wide as the panel, 3:2;
  `thumb_window`'s span, centring and resize rules apply along y — the active photo stays centred while
  the strip is resized, the fix of 2026-09-29.

### Placement

Lib `layout.rs`; `settings` (the field); `menu.rs` (commands, `MenuState`, `checked`, the View items).
Bin: a small `side_panel.rs` (`side(Side, id) -> egui::Panel`); `collections_panel.rs`,
`develop/mod.rs`, `develop/panel/mod.rs`, `develop/rail.rs` wiring; `filmstrip.rs` the axis;
`menu_bar.rs`, `native_menu.rs`, `commands.rs`, `shell`. `docs/DESIGN.md` §6.

## Tests

- **Lib:** the default; an empty settings file loads it; `develop_order` for all six panel/strip pairs;
  `checked` for each command; the View menu lists the items in order.
- **Drawn:** for each of the six Develop layouts, a frame of `develop::body` puts the tool rail, a panel
  slider and a filmstrip cell where the layout says — the strip outermost on a shared side, below the
  photo column at the bottom — with no two pieces overlapping; the Library rail on either side of the
  grid; the side strip draws only its visible window, a click selects and opens, and resizing it keeps
  the active photo where it was.
- **Commands:** each toggles the setting and saves it; choosing a place shows a hidden strip.
- **Sabotages, each red:** a shared side not putting the strip outermost; the strip ignoring its axis;
  the rail ignoring its side; `checked` always `None`; a place chosen without showing the strip.
