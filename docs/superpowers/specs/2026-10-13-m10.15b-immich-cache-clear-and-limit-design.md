# M10.15b — Clear the Immich downloads, and a soft limit that warns at startup

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Branch:** `m10.15b-immich-cache-clear` · **Status:** design approved

## Why

Opening an Immich photo, or pressing **Download**, keeps a full copy of the original in
`<cache dir>/rustroom/immich/<server>/`, and nothing ever removes those copies except the manual
**Remove downloads** on a selection (slice 4). A cached RAW is a second copy of a photo already on the
server — fine for a working set, a problem if the cache quietly grows into a library: one album
Download of RAFs at ~30 MB each runs to gigabytes.

Measured on the author's Mac (2026-10-13): **74 MB** — 21 originals (one 31 MB RAF, one 15 MB JPG), each
with an edits sidecar, plus 8 sidecars whose originals were already removed (by design). 97 GB free of
926 GB.

## Decisions (the author's)

- ⛔ **No automatic eviction.** The milestone was "a budget and eviction for cached originals"; the
  author re-scoped it to a button and a warning. Rustroom never deletes a download on its own.
- **Clear cache removes the downloaded originals only** — not the list cache (1.6 MB), not the Immich
  thumbnails (their own store, with its own slider and Clear already), not rendered previews.
- **A soft limit**, set in Settings, **default 10 GB**: past it, a **dialog at startup** offers to clear.
- **The warning can be switched off** — in Settings, and from the dialog itself.

## Behaviour

### Settings → Immich

Below the existing *Thumbnail cache* section, a section **Downloaded originals**:

- A size line: **"74.0 MB · 21 photos"** ("1 photo" in the singular), measured when the Settings window opens (like the thumbnail
  row's size, never per frame), and re-measured after a clear.
- **Clear…** — opens the dialog below (not an immediate delete: an original costs a real download to
  get back, a thumbnail does not).
- A slider **"GB limit"**, 1–1000, logarithmic, whole GB, default **10**.
- A tick box **"Warn at startup when downloads exceed the limit"**, default **on**.

Each control has a one-sentence hover (`docs/DESIGN.md` §8). The limit and the tick box are part of the
Settings draft: they land on **Apply**, like every other field. **Clear…** acts at once, through its
dialog, like the thumbnail row's Clear.

### The dialog — one dialog, two ways in

| | At startup, over the limit | From **Clear…** in Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Title | "Immich downloads use 12.4 GB" | "Clear Immich downloads" |
| Body | "Over your 10 GB limit. Clearing removes 412 downloaded photos; edits, marks and thumbnails stay, and a photo downloads again when you open it" | the same sentence without "Over your 10 GB limit." |
| Foot | **Don't warn again** (quiet, left) · **Not now** · **Clear cache** (primary) | **Cancel** · **Clear cache** (primary) |

- Standard width (520, §7.8) — the body is one long sentence; Escape cancels, Return presses
  **Clear cache**.
- **Don't warn again** unticks the setting, saves it, and closes the dialog without clearing.
- Opened from Settings, closing it — either button — **returns to the Settings window with its draft
  intact** (the `SaveExportPreset` pattern).
- With nothing downloaded, the Settings line reads "Nothing downloaded" and **Clear…** is disabled.

### What Clear cache does

Exactly what **Remove downloads** does, applied to every downloaded original of the configured server,
through the same `RustroomApp::remove_downloaded`:

- Deletes only the image files. **Sidecars (edits), catalog rows (marks) and thumbnails stay**, so a
  re-download — to the byte-identical path `cache_path` derives — reattaches everything.
- If the photo open in Develop is among them, its edits are persisted first and Develop is emptied.
- The grid's ↓ and the rail's count refresh at once; the status row says
  "Removed 412 local copies — they are still on Immich" (the existing message).
- **The server is never touched** — the browse key is read-only.

### At startup

After the app is built, and only when **Immich is connected** (`immich_source::connection` is `Some`:
the integration on and a server configured), **the warning is on**, and the downloads are **over** the
limit — strictly: exactly at the limit does not warn — the dialog opens.

Measuring is one `read_dir` of the server's cache directory: **~3 ms for 1,000 photos and 500 sidecars**
(measured 2026-10-13), so it runs synchronously.

### With Immich switched off

Nothing of this shows — no section, no startup check — consistent with the integration's "no mention at
all" rule. Clearing waits until it is switched on again. (The downloads stay visible as "Downloaded
photos" in Folders, as today.)

## Design

### `rustroom_app::immich_cache` (lib, no egui)

- `Usage { bytes: u64, photos: usize }` and **`usage(root: &Path) -> Usage`** — one `read_dir`,
  counting only files `catalog::is_supported` accepts: the same test `remove_downloads` and
  `immich_source::present_names` use, so what is measured, what is removed and what the rail counts
  cannot disagree. A missing directory is `Usage::default()`.
- **`downloads(root: &Path) -> Vec<PathBuf>`** — every supported image in the root, for the clear.
- **`limit_bytes(gb: u32) -> u64`** — `gb × 10⁹`: decimal, the units `photo_info::format_bytes`
  prints, so "12.4 GB" and "10 GB limit" are in the same unit.
- **`warn_at_startup(usage: Usage, limit_gb: u32, warn: bool) -> bool`** — `warn && usage.bytes >
  limit_bytes(limit_gb)`.
- The dialog's title and body as functions of `(usage, limit, startup)` — `dialog_title`,
  `dialog_body` — so their wording is testable without a window.

### `Settings`

Two fields, `immich_cache_limit_gb: u32` (**10**) and `immich_cache_warn: bool` (**true**). ⚠ No
field-level `#[serde(default)]`: the struct's container-level default supplies `Default`'s values for a
file that lacks them — a field-level one would give `0` and `false`, a limit every launch exceeds and a
warning silently off (the trap `thumb_budget_mb` documents).

⚠ In passing: the docs on `immich_key` and `immich_upload_key` still say "stored in plaintext… the OS
keychain remains out of scope" — false since M10.15a; corrected to point at `secrets`.

### The window (bin)

- **`modals/settings_immich.rs`** (new) — the Immich tab, **moved** out of `modals/settings.rs` (867 lines
  of code and doc, near the 1,000 ceiling) the way `settings_keyboard` and `settings_layout` were, plus
  the new section. `settings.rs` drops to ~720.
- **`modals/immich_cache.rs`** (new) — the dialog. `Modal::ImmichCache { startup: bool, usage: Usage }`;
  `startup: false` returns to `Modal::Settings` on close.
- **`immich_flow.rs`** — `clear_immich_cache()` (`downloads` → `remove_downloaded`) and
  `check_immich_cache_at_startup()`, called once after bootstrap.
- `app.rs` gains the Settings line's measured `Option<Usage>`, reset to `None` after a clear so the next
  look re-measures — the `immich_thumb_cache_bytes` shape.

## Tests

- **`usage` and `downloads` count images only** — a fixture with an image, its `.json` sidecar, a
  `.part` and a `.DS_Store`, each asserted to exist first so the test can fail; a missing directory is
  zero.
- **`warn_at_startup`'s edges** — at the limit: no; one byte over: yes; over with the warning off: no.
- **The wording** — the startup title names the size, the body names the limit only at startup, and
  the count ("1 downloaded photo" in the singular); the body names "edits" in both forms, so a
  reworded body cannot drop the reassurance.
- **Settings** — a file without the two fields loads `10` and `true`; a round trip keeps both.
- **Through the app harness** (`test_app`):
  - Clear cache removes every original, keeps every sidecar, and refreshes `immich_present`.
  - With the open photo among them, Develop is emptied and its edits reach the sidecar.
  - Startup opens the dialog only when connected, warning on, and over the limit — three negative
    cases (integration off, warning off, under the limit) plus the positive one.
  - In the dialog: **Clear cache** deletes; **Not now** deletes nothing; **Don't warn again** unticks
    the setting and saves it; from Settings, closing returns to `Modal::Settings` with the draft intact.
- The new controls' hovers pass `every_hover_is_one_short_sentence`; the moved tab keeps every existing
  Settings test green (`the_upload_key_survives_apply_and_leaves_the_browse_key_alone` among them).

## Out of scope

- ⛔ **Automatic eviction** — declined by the author for this milestone (above).
- Caches of a server no longer configured (only the configured server's directory is measured and
  cleared); the list cache; thumbnails (they already have their own limit and Clear).
- Deleting downloads when the integration is switched off (it stays "hide", as decided in slice 4's
  design).
