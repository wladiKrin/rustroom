# M10.16b — AI denoise: the design

**Item:** *"AI denoise (P-23, 2026-09-28). L"* — nothing more on record; P-23: *"The guided-filter NR is
pre-AI; `ai-mask` already brings `tract` and a model registry, so a denoise model is the natural next
tenant."*

## Decisions taken in the brainstorm (the author's)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Purpose | **Both** — high-ISO rescue (ISO 3200+) and everyday cleanup (ISO 800–2500) |
| Form | **An Amount slider**: the network runs once per photo in the background, cached; Amount blends original and denoised instantly; today's NR sliders stay |
| Approach | **DRUNet on the GPU (`wgpu`)** — our own shaders, no new dependency, every platform; staged behind a working CPU path |
| Strength | **Auto σ + Amount** — Rustroom measures the photo's noise and picks σ; no Strength slider |
| Cache | **Disk, soft limit** — per photo; Settings shows the total with Clear… and a GB limit warned at startup; nothing evicted on its own (the Immich downloads' rules, M10.15b) |
| Split | **b1** the `tract` path end to end (slow, the fallback) · **b2** the GPU engine (the speed) — Lens Blur's d1/d2 precedent |

## The spike that chose the network (2026-10-15)

Two candidates downloadable as ONNX with permissive licences, both run through `tract` 0.23.8 on 512 × 512
crops of the author's RAFs (DSCF6990 and DSCF6993 at ISO 3200, DSCF7194 at ISO 1600), shadow, detail and
centre crops, beside today's NR (Luminance 40, Color 50):

- **SCUNet "real"** (`scunet_color_real_psnr_512.onnx`, Apache-2.0, 74.4 MB, `artiva-ai/modelos`) —
  **does not load**: `tract` panics in `ScatterND` (`index < dim`) while typing the graph, optimised or
  not. Out unless the runtime changes.
- **DRUNet colour** (`drunet_color.onnx`, 130,589,232 bytes, SHA-256
  `2ae3ab5eb15daac2ee79be984d584b908ce7f0f60b27be87d005f728c2aa0087`, `synthscript/drunet-color-onnx`) —
  input `[b, 4, h, w]`: sRGB display-referred RGB in 0…1 plus a constant plane σ/255; dynamic size,
  divisible by 8. **~4.2 s a 512² tile** on the M2's CPU under load ≈ 8 → **~5–8 minutes for a 26 Mpx
  frame** (117 tiles). Intrinsic: ~120 trillion operations a frame.
- **The look:** today's NR leaves most of the grain. **σ 8 cleans both ISO 1600 skin and ISO 3200
  shadows and keeps the leading in stained glass and most hair; σ 16 goes waxy** — thin strands vanish.
  The right σ matters more than anything else.
- NAFNet-SIDD (MIT, ~10× lighter) has no ONNX export; `onnxruntime` was measured 2.7–2.8× faster than
  `tract` in M9.13 and CoreML could not compile BiRefNet then.

**Licence:** DRUNet's weights are MIT (Kai Zhang, KAIR v1.0). ⚠ Its training data included DIV2K, whose
own terms say *research purposes only*; whether that reaches the weights is unsettled — the registry's
licence note says so, as M9.13's did for IS-Net.

## §1 What the user sees and does

- **Detail → "AI denoise"** above today's sliders: **Amount 0–100**, off at 0 (`Edits.ai_denoise:
  AiDenoise { amount }`, `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "AiDenoise::is_default")]` — off is never
  written). Its group is **Detail's** (it travels with Paste / Sync / presets like the NR sliders); a pasted
  photo computes when it is opened or exported.
- Amount above 0 on a photo whose result is missing **starts a background job** on the full-resolution
  decode. The block says *"Denoising… 37 %"*; the photo stays editable; the result appears when it lands.
  The job waits for the full decode (the session's buffer is the half-size one until it lands) — the line
  says *"Waiting for the full-resolution decode…"* meanwhile.
- **Today's Luminance / Detail / Color / Smoothness stay** and act on the denoised result for fine-tuning.
- **Export** uses the cached result; when the export's demosaic quality differs from the one it was computed
  on, the export computes its own (slow) and its status says so.
- **Settings → Performance** (the tab that already shows the caches, the GPU and the AI subject model): the
  denoise results' total with **Clear…** and a **GB limit** (default 10) warned at startup, switchable off —
  the M10.15b dialogs and rules. Nothing is evicted on its own. ⚠ Its own module: `modals/settings.rs` holds
  863 lines of code against the 1,000 ceiling (the Immich tab's M10.15b precedent).
- A missing model opens the existing download dialog (`ModelId::DrunetColor`, 131 MB).

## §2 The processing

- **The encoding the network sees.** The full decode (linear ProPhoto) → linear sRGB primaries → scaled by
  `0.9 / L₉₉.₅` (the 99.5th-percentile luminance on a fixed grid, `lens_blur::highlight_level`'s rule) →
  the sRGB transfer → clamped to 0…1.
- **A correction, not a replacement.** The network's change `d − e` in that encoding is turned back into
  linear ProPhoto (`lin(d) − lin(e)`, each through the inverse transfer, scale and primaries) and added to
  the ORIGINAL pixel. Where nothing clipped this is exactly `lin(d)`; clipped highlights and out-of-gamut
  colours — which the network saw clamped — keep their real values plus a correction of ≈ 0. The result
  never loses headroom.
- **The automatic σ.** A robust noise estimate in the same encoding: the median absolute deviation of the
  finest-scale detail (a 2 × 2 Haar diagonal) over the flattest blocks of the frame, × 1.4826, × 255 →
  σ in DRUNet's units, clamped to 1…40. Calibrated in b1's spike against the author's eye (§4).
- **Tiles.** 512 × 512 with a 32 px overlap, blended linearly across it — no seams, memory bounded at any
  frame size, progress per tile. Each tile's input is padded to a multiple of 8 by reflection.
- **The cache.** One file per photo: the corrected full-resolution image as half-floats, compressed
  (`zstd`, measured in b1), under the cache folder, keyed on the photo's path and size+mtime, the demosaic
  tier, the film negative (`ai_cache::key`'s rule), the model and σ. A content id (`AiMaps`'s rule) goes into
  the step's key.
- **The step.** The prefix's FIRST step, before lens correction — while each pixel's noise is its own. It
  reads the cached result at the frame's size (a `RasterSource`-like lazy plane, M10.16a's pattern: the
  prefix has not produced the frame when the step is built) and blends it with its input by Amount. Its key:
  `PrefixStepKey::AiDenoise(amount bits, result id)` — an Amount drag re-runs the blend only.
- **Where the decode is half-size** (the edit buffer before the full decode is adopted) no step is built and
  the job has not started; once the full decode is the session's buffer the result lines up pixel for pixel.

## §3 The GPU engine (b2) and the split

- **DRUNet is plain convolutions:** UNetRes — a 3 × 3 head (4 → 64), four scales of four residual blocks
  (`x + conv(relu(conv(x)))`, bias-free 3 × 3) with 2 × 2 stride-2 down- and transposed up-convolutions
  (64 → 128 → 256 → 512 and back), additive skips, a 3 × 3 tail (64 → 3). No attention.
- **The engine:** three convolution kernels — 3 × 3, 2 × 2 down, 2 × 2 transposed up — each computing a
  block of output channels per workgroup with the input patch staged in workgroup memory; ReLU and the
  residual add fused into the 3 × 3 kernel's store. Weights read once from the ONNX file's initializers
  (tract's own parser) and uploaded once per session; activations resident per tile.
- **Held to `tract`:** a parity test per kernel and over the whole network on small and odd-sized tiles,
  within a tolerance b1 measures; full-frame times on the author's three photos. **Estimate: 1–2 minutes a
  photo.**
- **b1 (first merge):** the `tract` path end to end — the model in the registry, the noise estimate, the
  tiled job with progress, the cache with its Settings, the prefix step, the Amount slider, export. Correct,
  slow (5–8 minutes a photo), and the fallback without a GPU adapter.
- **b2 (second merge):** the GPU engine swapped in as the job's runner whenever an adapter exists — the same
  picture, faster.

## §4 The spike and the tests

**b1, Task 1 — a spike, kept as an example** (`examples/denoise_probe` growing from this brainstorm's two
throwaway halves): the noise estimate on the three RAFs against sheets at σ 4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 12, shown to
the author with the estimate marked; a full frame through `tract` timed; the cache file's size at
half-float + zstd measured. **Rules:** the estimate within ~2 σ of the author's pick on all three photos, or
recalibrate before building on it; a cache file over ~150 MB → reconsider the format.

**Tests (b1)** — every key test sabotaged once, an inert sabotage recorded as inert:
- the encoding: identity where nothing clips; clipped and out-of-gamut values pass through unchanged;
- the noise estimate: synthetic Gaussian noise of a known σ reads within 10 %; a clean gradient ≈ 0;
- the tiling: seamless — a network that adds a constant gives a constant result across every overlap;
- the step: Amount 0 → no step, identical pixels; no result yet → no step and the waiting line; the key moves
  with Amount and the result's id; the prefix step-counting tests renumbered (or handed a result, CLAUDE.md's
  warning about steps that wait for maps);
- the cache: write, find, clear; the soft limit warns; the edit-model field: off never written, its group,
  `merge_from`, the three exhaustive fixtures; the slider resets; every hover passes the sweep;
- goldens: a synthetic noisy chart with a stored denoised result, at Amount 50 and 100.

**Tests (b2):** parity per kernel and over the whole network (64 × 64, 301 × 203 tiles); full-frame times.
**Rule:** a whole frame over ~3 minutes → tune the kernels before merging.

## Out of scope

- A Strength slider (σ by hand) — the author chose automatic σ; re-runs would cost minutes.
- Raw-domain (pre-demosaic) denoising — published raw networks expect a Bayer pattern; the X-T4 is X-Trans.
- Per-mask AI denoise — the existing per-mask NR stays; AI denoise is global.
- A second runtime (`onnxruntime`, CoreML) — declined for the GPU port.
