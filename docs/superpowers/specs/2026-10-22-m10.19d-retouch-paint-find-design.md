# M10.19d — The Retouch panel as two tasks: Paint · Find

## Intent

The author, after M10.16c3 (2026-10-22): *"we have several buttons, like detect objects, refresh, find people, find
dust, remove dust, and it's all in one place, and it's really hard to understand what is happening here. Also, removal
and patches seem to be similar in what they do, but they are kept separately … the fact that you can draw again after
clicking find people to search in a specific area is not clear at all from the controls."* He picked **option B** of
the canvas `https://claude.ai/artifact/7ACHJJ2zFpWPJ3WspdRYcC` and asked for M10.16c3 to be merged first.

What goes wrong today, read from the code: three lists (Patches, Removals, Eyes); five modes in one row (Remove ·
Heal · Clone · Red eye · Pet eye); under them, by mode, Detect objects, the selected removal's Opacity and Refresh,
Find people and its review; then, in every mode, the Find dust view with its Threshold and Remove dust with its
Sensitivity. Five things start work, three different ways, and what a drag on the photo does — paint a removal,
place a patch, add a person — follows state nothing on screen names.

## The design (option B — the author's pick)

**One switch decides what the photo does.** At the top of the Retouch panel, a segmented control at the primary
size with an icon each: **Paint · Find**. Paint: a drag retouches with the brush. Find: a click picks or unpicks
what was found, and a drag — when **Add more** is chosen — asks to look again there. Under the switch, only that
task's controls; under those, the one list of everything retouched, in both tasks.

### Paint

- **Brush** card: a segmented **Remove · Heal · Clone · Eye**; under it the brush's settings for *new* retouches:
  - Remove: **Size**, **Whole object** (today's Detect objects, renamed for what it does).
  - Heal and Clone: **Size**, **Feather**.
  - Eye: a compact segmented **Red eye · Pet eye**, then the eye tool's settings as today.
- The photo: today's overlays by brush — the Remove overlay, the patch overlay, the eye overlay (eyes drawn in every
  brush, gestures only in Eye, as now).

### Find

- **Find** card: a compact segmented **People · Dust** (what to look for), then **Find people** / **Find dust**
  (**Find again** once something has been found). For Dust, its **Sensitivity** slider sits above the button.
- While a search runs: its quiet progress line (DESIGN §7.16) — "Looking for people", "Looking for dust",
  "Looking there".
- After a search, the **review**:
  - the readout — "12 people · 9 to remove", "7 spots · 7 to remove", "No people found", "No dust found";
  - **On the photo:** a segmented **Pick · Add more** — Pick: a click toggles one; Add more: a drawing (the brush's
    size) looks again in the area around it, closer, and adds who or what it finds there, ticked — else, the
    author's (a), what is under the drawing. **This is where drawing to look again becomes visible.** Pick after
    every search;
  - **Everyone** (all, none, mixed);
  - **Remove 9 people** / **Remove 7 spots** — the card's primary button — and **Cancel**.
- **Dust is reviewed the way people are** (new): Find dust shows the spots found on the photo, each a shaded circle
  with a light border, all ticked; a click unticks; Add more looks there at the highest sensitivity, else takes the
  drawing itself as a spot. Remove N spots makes them Heal patches, one undo step — today's Remove dust, reviewed
  first. While dust is reviewed, the **dust view** (today's Find dust, the high-contrast view) is on, with a **Dust
  view** checkbox and its **Contrast** slider (today's Threshold) in the review, off again when the review ends.
- People's review is M10.16c3's, unchanged inside: pre-tick under 35 % of the frame's height, the border along each
  outline, look there, "No one new there".
- Find before any search: the photo shows the retouches' outlines as Paint does, and a click or drag does nothing.

### The one list — **Retouches**

- One card under the task's controls, in both tasks: every retouch, grouped by kind in a fixed order — removals
  (a stroke's "Removal N", a person's "Person N"), patches ("Heal N", "Clone N", "Dust N"), eyes ("Red eye N",
  "Pet eye N") — each kind numbered on its own, each row with a kind icon, its eye (show the outline), Hold out and
  ×, as the three lists have today. The header: "Retouches", the count, Reset (all of them, one undo step).
  **Show all / Hide all** under the list applies to every kind.
- **One selection across kinds.** A row picked selects that retouch and nothing else; Paint becomes the task and the
  brush the retouch's own (Remove for a removal or person, Heal / Clone for a patch, Eye for an eye), so the photo
  shows it with its handles — today's rule for patches and removals, now for all.
- **The selected retouch's settings sit under the list** (a sub-label with its name): a removal or person —
  **Opacity**, **Try another fill** (today's Refresh); a patch — **Heal · Clone** (switches it, as today), **Size**,
  **Feather**; an eye — its settings. ⚠ The brush card then only ever sets new retouches — today a selected patch
  silently took over the brush's sliders.
- **Dust N** needs one fact today's data lacks: which patches Remove dust made. `edit_model::Spot` gains
  `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "is_false")] dust: bool` — no saved photo's serialization changes, and a
  patch's rendering ignores it.

### Words

| Today | Now |
|---|---|
| Detect objects | Whole object |
| Refresh | Try another fill |
| Find dust (the view) | Dust view, inside dust's review |
| Remove dust | Find · Dust → Remove N spots |
| (no control) | Pick · Add more |
| Patches / Removals / Eyes | Retouches |
| Red eye · Pet eye (modes) | Eye, then Red eye · Pet eye |

Every new control gets its one hover sentence (DESIGN §8).

## How it works

- **The task** is the app's, beside the brush tools: `rustroom_app::retouch_task::Task { Paint, Find }` and
  `FindKind { People, Dust }`, remembered for the run, Paint by default.
- **The list** is computed, never stored: `rustroom_app::retouch_list` (lib) maps an `Edits` to its rows — kind,
  index into its own vector, label — and a selection to the retouch it names, so the grouping, the numbering and the
  selection across kinds are unit-tested without a window. The rows' flags stay where they are (`spot_shown`,
  `spot_held`, `RemovalUi`'s, the eyes').
- **Dust's review** extends `dust_tool::Run` with the finds, which are ticked, the view's state and a look-there
  request — the same shape as `people_tool::Run` — and `dust_flow` lands a search as a review instead of patches.
  Remove N spots runs today's `to_spots` over the ticked finds, the patches marked `dust`.
- **The canvas** dispatches on the task: Find → the find overlay (people's, generalised to dust: shaded circles, a
  light border, the same click and Add-more gestures, chosen by **Pick · Add more** instead of click-versus-drag);
  Paint → today's overlays by brush.
- **Files.** `retouch.rs` sits at the line ceiling; the panel is rebuilt by responsibility: `retouch.rs` (the task
  switch and the order), `retouch_brush.rs`, `retouch_find.rs` (both kinds' review), `retouch_list.rs` (the one list
  and the selected retouch's settings). Today's `retouch_remove`, `retouch_people` and `retouch_dust` fold into
  those; `retouch_eyes` keeps the eye settings.

## Not in M10.19d

- Changing a retouch's *kind* after the fact (a removal into a heal, as Lightroom allows) — removals live in the
  decode, patches after the lens; that is a data-model change, its own item if wanted.
- A list of the people found in the panel (option C's) — the photo is the list; added later if crowds need it.
- Reflections (M10.16c4).

## Testing

- `retouch_list`: rows grouped and numbered per kind (a stroke and a person numbered apart; heal, clone and dust
  apart); a selection maps to exactly one retouch; deleting a row's retouch renumbers its kind only.
- The task switch: Find hides the brush and shows the find card; picking a list row returns to Paint with the row's
  brush; the photo's gestures follow the task (a drag in Find paints nothing; in Paint it paints).
- Find: People and Dust each search, review, Remove N (one undo step) and Cancel (none); **Pick · Add more** —
  in Pick a drag asks for nothing, in Add more a drag asks to look there; Pick again after each search.
- Dust's review: the finds shown, all ticked; a click unticks; Remove N spots makes N Heal patches marked `dust`,
  listed "Dust N"; the dust view is on while reviewing and off after.
- The selected retouch's settings edit that retouch only; the brush's settings never touch a selected one.
- The hover sweep and the coverage walk over the new panel; every existing Retouch behaviour's test kept, moved to
  where its control now lives.
