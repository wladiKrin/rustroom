# M10.18b: GPS and a map, design

**Status:** written 2026-10-28, from a short conversation with the author.

- **The author's words:**
  - **Option A** of the mockups (<https://claude.ai/artifact/VSLcsvSFuZnebRxHXzgLzj>). "Add C on top
    later if needed."
  - Located photos come from both the phone through Immich and the X-T4 tagged through the
    Fujifilm app.
  - The map is for finding the photos from one place and for seeing where one shot was taken.
  - Photos are placed "by dragging by hand, or putting into a location field … for several photos
    at once".
  - "Sounds good" to keeping Immich's locations Rustroom's own. Then, on the builder check's
    sidecar question: "should we maybe move the raw locations also to the catalog, for
    consistency?" A hand-set location therefore lives **in the catalog only**, for every photo.
- **Everything marked *my call*** is open to correction.

## Intent

Photos already carry where they were taken: the X-T4 writes the phone's position into each file,
and Immich keeps one for every phone photo. Rustroom throws it away today. The milestone gives a
Library a **Map** view. It shows where the photos in view were taken, lets you pick out the ones
from one place, and lets you put photos without a location on the map by hand, many at once.

Success is when the author opens a trip folder, presses Map, sees clusters over the places he
went, clicks Lisbon to get those shots, and then drags the 30 untagged frames from the day trip
onto Sintra.

## Goals

1. **Read locations.**
   - A file's EXIF GPS: a RAW through libraw (`rsraw::GpsInfo`), JPEG, TIFF and PNG through
     `image-decode`'s EXIF reader (`kamadak-exif`).
   - An Immich asset's `exifInfo.latitude` and `longitude`.
2. **A Map view in the Library,** the fourth view beside Grid, Compare and Survey (mockup A).
   - It shows whatever the Library shows: a folder, an album, a keyword, All Photos, an Immich
     view.
   - Photos are **clustered pins with counts**, splitting as you zoom.
   - **Click a cluster** and its photos fill a strip along the bottom, named by the place when
     one is known. A **double-click** on a strip photo opens it in Develop.
   - **The selection follows.** Photos selected in the Grid are outlined on the map. Switching
     to Map with one photo selected centres on it.
   - The status row reads "326 of 412 photos have a location".
3. **Place photos by hand, many at once:**
   - **Drag** a selection, from the strip or from the filmstrip, onto the map. The drop point is
     their location.
   - A **location field** in the Map view: type a place ("Sintra"), pick from the matches, and
     the selection takes it. "38.80, -9.38" typed directly also works.
   - **Remove location** for the selection.
   - Each of these is **one undo step**.
4. **Save a hand-set location in the catalog**, for every kind of photo alike (the author's call,
   for consistency). No sidecar is written for it, and **the original file is never written.**
   - The cost, accepted: darktable or Lightroom don't see a location set in Rustroom.
   - A sidecar writer for RAWs can come later if it's missed.
   - **Exports carry the location** (my call): with the export's "All metadata" the exported
     file's metadata holds the position, and "Copyright only" drops it, the setting for posting
     online. This joins `export::metadata`'s scope.
5. **The map itself:**
   - OpenStreetMap raster tiles, fetched in the background and **cached on disk**, so a place
     already seen works offline.
   - The "© OpenStreetMap contributors" credit is always shown.
   - The tile server is a setting, so the author can point it at his own.

## Non-goals (this milestone)

- **Option C:** the small Location card in Develop. Later, if the author wants it.
- **Option B:** a Places tree in the rail with offline place names.
- **Writing a location to Immich.** It stays Rustroom's, as Rustroom's own stars do.
  - A "send location" would join Send marks later.
  - Never in this milestone, because Rustroom writes to Immich only from Send marks'
    confirmation (CLAUDE.md, Immich invariants).
- **Matching photos to a GPS track file (GPX) by time.** It's Lightroom's other way of
  placing photos, and the author asked only for drag and the field. It's named here so it isn't
  forgotten.
- **Reverse geocoding every photo to a place name in the background.** Only the strip's title
  and the field's matches use names.
- **Altitude and direction.**

## Anti-goals

- **A rail or grid that slows with the catalog.** The Library rail warning in CLAUDE.md applies
  to the map too.
  - Positions are read from the catalog once per change, never per frame.
  - Clustering is a grid bucket per zoom level over the view's points, memoised on the view's
    content and the zoom.
  - The 100k-photo scale test (`catalog_scale`) must not move.
- **Breaking OpenStreetMap's tile usage policy.**
  - A real User-Agent naming Rustroom, at most 2 tile downloads at once, and the disk cache
    honoured.
  - Never prefetching areas the view isn't showing.
  - Nominatim, the place search, at most 1 request a second, only on Enter or after typing
    stops, with the User-Agent too.
- **A hand-set location silently overwritten.** The rule for which source wins is below, and a
  rescan never changes it.
- **Rewriting a file Rustroom doesn't own.** A location is never written to a file or a
  sidecar.

## Which location a photo has

Three sources, kept apart in the catalog so each can be cleared or refreshed on its own:

| Source | Written by | Wins over |
|---|---|---|
| `hand` | drag, the field, Remove (a removal is `hand` with no position) | everything |
| `hand` | also: a GPS found in another program's `.xmp` sidecar (`import_newer`), *my call*, only where no hand row exists. A sidecar without GPS never deletes a hand row | — |
| `file` | the background metadata scan (`metadata_scanner`), from the file's EXIF | `server` |
| `server` | Immich's `exifInfo`, at the same fill as stars (`fill_from_server`) | nothing |

- **Remove location** records "hand: none". A photo whose file has GPS then shows no location,
  which is what the author asked for. Clearing the hand row brings the file's back.
- **Undo** restores the hand row as it was.

## How it is built (my calls)

- **Catalog (schema v15):** a per-file table `photo_location (folder_id, file_name, source,
  lat, lon)`, keyed like the others. It joins `PER_FILE_TABLES`, so moves and relinks carry it.
  - `Catalog::locations_of(paths)` answers with the winning position per photo.
  - `set_hand_location(paths, Option<(lat, lon)>)` writes the hand rows.
  - `clear_hand_location(paths)` removes them.
- **`core-image::Capture`** gains `gps: Option<(f64, f64)>`, decimal degrees with south and west
  negative. Both decoders fill it.
  - libraw reports "no fix" as zeros with `gpsparsed` still set (`rsraw`'s own note). (0, 0)
    therefore means no location.
  - The scanner writes `file` rows.
- **`immich::ExifInfo`** gains nullable `latitude` and `longitude`.
  - ⚠ CLAUDE.md's rule: capture a real asset reply from the author's server before trusting
    the DTO. Both fields are `null` on photos without GPS.
- **`xmp`:** `Sidecar.gps: Option<(f64, f64)>`, **read only**, parsing XMP's `DDD,MM.mmmmN`
  form, so another program's location is picked up. Rustroom never writes it.
- **Photos read before this milestone** (the builder check's #1, *my call*):
  - `files_without_capture` queues only unread files, so every folder already opened would
    show no pins.
  - A **one-time background pass at startup** reads only the GPS of every catalogued file
    that has no location read yet: the EXIF block, a few ms each.
  - It is marked per file (`photo_location`'s `file` row, or a "read, none" marker), so it runs
    once and is resumable.
  - It is paced behind the existing metadata scan and must not touch the UI thread.
- **Lib, no egui:**
  - `geo::{mercator, tiles}`: lat/lon ↔ world pixels at a zoom, which tiles cover a view, a
    tile's cache path.
  - `map_cluster`: grid bucketing, memoised.
  - `place_search`: Nominatim's query URL, and parsing its reply into `(name, lat, lon)`. The
    fixture must be a captured reply.
    - **Searches only on Enter** (*my call*): Nominatim's policy forbids search-as-you-type
      on its public server.
- **A tile worker** like the Immich worker: background threads with `ureq`, a disk cache under
  `~/.cache/rustroom/tiles/<server>/z/x/y.png`, and an in-memory LRU of textures. A tile not yet
  there draws as the `canvas` colour.
- **Bin:**
  - `library/map_view.rs`: pan by drag, zoom by scroll and pinch, the + and − buttons, pins and
    clusters drawn by the theme's accent, the strip, and the drop target for a dragged
    selection.
  - The location field sits in the view's top-right.
  - `Grid · Compare · Survey · Map` in the top row's segment; the Library's view enum gains
    `Map`.
  - **Shortcut:** none by default (my call: M is Panorama's ⇧⌘M neighbour only with modifiers,
    and the bare letters are tools). It's bindable in Settings → Keyboard.
- **Settings:** `map_tile_url`, default `https://tile.openstreetmap.org/{z}/{x}/{y}.png`, in
  Settings → General.
- **Hover text:** one sentence, no full stop. **Design:** a new element follows DESIGN.md §12.
  The map's land and water come from the tiles, never from theme colours, and the pins use the
  accent, because a selected or set state is the accent's job.

## Testing

- **Readers:**
  - A RAW's GPS through libraw, using a fixture with GPS. If the repo's test RAFs have none,
    the raw path is proven on the author's real X-T4 file in the GUI check. That's said
    honestly, not faked.
  - A JPEG's EXIF GPS, from a constructed EXIF block. "No fix" zeros read as none.
- **Catalog:**
  - Hand beats file beats server.
  - Remove hides the file's position, and clearing the hand row brings it back.
  - A move carries the rows (`PER_FILE_TABLES`).
  - A v14 catalog migrates.
- **XMP:** parsing `DDD,MM.mmmmN` in all four hemispheres.
  - A sidecar with GPS fills a hand row only where none exists.
  - A sidecar without GPS keeps the hand row.
- **Startup pass:** it reads a GPS for a file catalogued before v15 exactly once, and a second
  start reads nothing.
- **Export:** "All metadata" writes the position, and "Copyright only" doesn't.
- **Geo:** the projection round-trips, the tiles covering a view are right at zooms 0, 5 and
  18, and the dateline wraps.
- **Clustering:** counts sum to the input, and the memo isn't re-run on a frame with no change.
  A 100k-point timing test sits beside `catalog_scale`.
- **Place search:** parsing a captured Nominatim reply, URL encoding, and the 1-a-second
  throttle (held behind a gate, not timed).
- **App, headless:**
  - Drop a selection of 3 on a point: three hand rows, one undo step, no sidecar written, and
    Undo restores the previous rows.
  - The field with "38.80, -9.38" places the selection.
  - The status count matches.
- **GUI check by the author:** tiles load and stay cached offline, the clusters at the trip, the
  strip, a drag onto Sintra, Undo, and an Immich phone photo's pin.
