# M10.18c: People, design

**Status:** written 2026-10-28, from a conversation with the author.

- **The author's words:**
  - Looks for people "usually on Immich".
  - Asked whether Lightroom has this.
  - "I'd also like to have a local face detection."
  - Lightroom's grouping of look-alike faces, named a stack at once: "yes".
  - Names "stay in rustroom only".
  - Layout **A** of the mockups (<https://claude.ai/artifact/8veXQc9qb5x7ivD4GnM5UW>): a People view beside Grid and Map.
  - On the builder check's first question, clicking an Immich person shows **all** their photos on the server: "first".
- **Everything marked *my call*** is open to correction.

## Intent

Find every photo of someone. Immich already knows the faces and names of everything on the server, and the author
uses it for exactly that. Rustroom should show those people, and do the same for local folders that never go to
Immich: find the faces itself, group the ones that look alike, and let the author name a whole group at once, as
Lightroom Classic's People view does.

Success is when the author opens a local shoot, presses People, sees "Who is this?" groups of the shoot's faces,
types "Anna" once for a group of 18, confirms "Ben?" for another, and from then on clicks Anna in the rail to see
every photo of her, from Immich and local folders alike.

## Goals

1. **One People list for the whole catalog,** in the rail (a new **People** section, names with counts) and in
   the People view's top row.
   - **Immich's people:** names and faces read from the server.
   - **Rustroom's own:** named here.
   - **The same name is the same person** (*my call*): a person named "Anna" in Rustroom joins Immich's "Anna".
2. **Click a person** (in the rail or the view) and the Library shows their photos.
   - **An Immich person** (the author's choice) is a server-backed source like an album: **all** their photos on
     the server, through the search with `personIds` and paged, with Immich's count. It's opened on the Immich
     worker with an album's refresh and failure states, and `Reindex` fetches it again.
   - **A person named only in Rustroom** is a catalog-backed source like a keyword's, following the catalog
     (`source_refresh`).
   - **A person in both** shows Immich's photos and the local ones together.
3. **Local face finding,** in the background, for the photos in view.
   - It starts when the People view is open on a source.
   - It skips photos already looked at, and photos from Immich, which have Immich's faces.
   - Progress shows in the status row with Cancel: "Looking for faces · 41 of 64 photos".
   - Settings → Behaviour has **Find faces in local photos** (on by default) to turn it off (*my call*).
4. **Groups of look-alike faces.** Unnamed faces in view are clustered by similarity.
   - Each group shows a stack of faces and a count.
   - **A name field.** Naming a group names all its faces.
   - **"Anna?"** with **Yes / No** when a group looks like someone already named. No moves the group to
     "not Anna", and it is never offered as Anna again.
   - Clicking a face shows the photos it's in. Removing one wrong face from a group is done from its photo's
     faces (below).
5. **A photo's faces:** in the People view, clicking a face opens its photo with every face's box. A **local** face
   can be renamed, cleared (back to unnamed), or deleted as "not a face".
   - **Immich's faces are shown, not edited** (*my call*). They are changed in Immich, and a refresh replaces them
     wholesale.
   - Immich's **unnamed** people and hidden ones do not appear.
6. **Stored in the catalog only.** Faces, their positions, their embeddings and names never go to a file, a sidecar
   or Immich (the author: "stay in rustroom only"). Immich's own people are read, never written.

## Non-goals

- Writing names to Immich, or to XMP face regions (`mwg-rs:Regions`). The latter would be possible later, as a
  separate choice.
- Recognising people in Develop's canvas. The faces show in the People view and its photo boxes only.
- Pets. Immich does them, but this milestone doesn't.
- A person keyword in the keyword tree, which is what Lightroom does. People are their own list here (*my call*),
  so the keyword tree isn't flooded.

## Anti-goals

- **A UI that stalls.**
  - Detection and embedding run on a worker thread with a decoded preview: the embedded JPEG or the 512-px
    thumbnail, never a full RAW decode.
  - Clustering runs off the UI thread and is memoised.
  - Nothing per frame grows with the catalog: the rail warning in CLAUDE.md applies, and the rail reads People
    through `RailData`.
- **A name lost or silently moved.**
  - A face the author named or confirmed is never renamed by a later clustering.
  - Clustering only ever suggests ("Anna?"). The one exception is naming a group, which the author does
    explicitly.
- **A licence problem.** No InsightFace weights (non-commercial).

## How it is built (my calls)

- **The picture faces are looked for in** (*my call*):
  - A RAW uses its embedded camera JPEG at its own size (4,416 px on an X-T4).
  - Any other file is decoded and downscaled to 2,048 px on the long edge.
  - ⚠ Never the 512-px thumbnail: a group shot's faces are 15–25 px there, too small for YuNet and for SFace's
    112×112 crop.
- **Names and Immich** (*my call*):
  - An Immich person is linked by its **id** (`people.immich_person_id`), so a rename in Immich follows.
  - A name typed in Rustroom joins the one Immich person of that name, case-insensitively, when exactly one
    exists. Otherwise it's a new local person.
  - Two Immich "Anna"s stay two people, so `people.name` is **not** UNIQUE.
  - The People list shows one entry per person, and two of the same name are told apart by a face.
- **"Ben?" suggestions** (*my call*) come only from people with **confirmed local** faces. Immich exposes no
  embeddings, and re-running SFace on Immich's face boxes (downloading thumbnails, re-detecting landmarks) is a
  later pipeline, out of scope here. A person known only from Immich is suggested once one local group is
  named after them.
- **The registry's size check:** `every_model_is_pinned_and_licensed` requires over 1 MB, and YuNet is about
  230 KB. Lower that bound for it, with a note.
- **Networks** (crate `ai-mask`, through `tract`, downloaded on first use through the existing registry and
  download flow):
  - **YuNet** (OpenCV Zoo, Apache-2.0) finds faces and five landmarks.
  - **SFace** (OpenCV Zoo, Apache-2.0) makes a 128-d embedding from the aligned 112×112 face.
  - ⚠ Verify both export through `tract` before anything else. If one doesn't, report it rather than swap in
    an unlicensed model.
- **Catalog (v16):**
  - `faces (id, folder_id, file_name, x, y, w, h, embedding BLOB, person_id NULL, source 'local'|'immich', immich_face_id NULL, confirmed INTEGER)`
    is a per-file table joining `PER_FILE_TABLES`. The box is normalised to the photo's upright frame.
  - `people (id, name COLLATE NOCASE, immich_person_id NULL UNIQUE)`.
  - `face_rejections (face_id, person_id)` records the "No" answers.
  - `faces_read (folder_id, file_name)` marks photos already looked at (per-file, in `PER_FILE_TABLES`).
- **Immich:**
  - The people list is `GET /people`.
  - Each asset's faces come from the search (`withPeople: true`) or `GET /faces?id=`.
  - Both are stored as `source = 'immich'` rows, refreshed when an album or month opens, as `fill_marks` is.
  - ⚠ Capture a real reply before writing the DTO (CLAUDE.md's rule). An API key may lack `person.read`: then
    Immich's people are just absent, said once in the status row.
- **Clustering** (lib, no egui):
  - Cosine similarity on SFace embeddings.
  - Unnamed faces within the threshold of each other form a group, by agglomerative linking at SFace's published
    cosine threshold (0.363), refined on real photos.
  - A group is offered as a named person when its mean embedding is within the threshold of that person's
    confirmed faces.
  - Memoised on the catalog's change count and the view.
- **The People view:** `LibraryMode::People`, a fifth segment beside Map, with its icon proven in the cmap.
  - The named row is face crops cut from the stored thumbnails.
  - The groups grid shows up to five faces stacked, the count, the name field (Enter) or **Yes / No**.
  - Hover text on every control.
- **Rail:** a People section, through `RailData` (the counts are one query per catalog change).
- **Undo:** naming, confirming, rejecting, clearing and deleting a face are each one undo step
  (`ActionKind::FaceChange`, the before and after `person_id`/state per face).

## Testing

- **Networks:**
  - YuNet finds the face in a fixture: a real photo the author agrees to commit, or a public-domain portrait.
    Its box and landmarks are within tolerance.
  - SFace's embeddings of two crops of one face are closer than two different faces.
  - Both are `#[ignore]`d when the models are absent, as the other AI tests are, with a note.
- **Catalog:**
  - The v16 migration.
  - Faces and names round-trip.
  - A move carries faces (`PER_FILE_TABLES`).
  - A rejection is never offered again.
  - Forget/restore carries faces.
- **Clustering:**
  - Constructed embeddings: two tight groups and an outlier give two groups.
  - Naming leaves confirmed faces alone.
  - The memo doesn't re-run on an unchanged frame.
- **Immich:** a captured `/people` reply and a captured asset reply with people parse. `person.read` missing
  reads as "no people".
- **App, headless:**
  - Naming a group of 3 gives three faces with Anna, one undo step, and the rail shows Anna 3.
  - Yes on "Anna?" works, and No is not offered again.
  - Clicking Anna shows her photos.
  - The background search lands faces for a folder (an injected finder, as `people_tool::Finders` does) and
    skips an Immich photo.
- **GUI check by the author:** a real shoot's groups, Immich's people in the rail, and naming and confirming.

## Left to the builder

- The face crop size in the view.
- How many faces a group shows.
- The order of groups: largest first is the default.
