# M10.18d — Publish services (design)

ahead", 2026-10-29). **Every choice below is the builder's call** unless marked as his; he checks them in use.

## Intent

Lightroom's *publish services* keep a set of photos **in step with a place**: drop photos into a published
collection, press Publish, and each is exported there; edit one later and it is marked *Modified* and
re-exported on the next Publish; take one out and its exported copy goes on the next Publish. Rustroom has
export (presets, a background worker, Immich upload) but nothing that **remembers what was exported where** —
that memory is the feature.

The author uses no web service. **Two destinations** (builder's call): a **folder on disk** (Lightroom's
"Hard Drive" service — a phone sync folder, a website's image folder, a shared drive) and an **Immich album**
(the one service he uses).

## Goals

1. **A publish service** = a name, a destination, and export settings. Created from the rail; edited
   (rename, settings, destination folder) from its right-click menu; deleted with one confirmation, which
   never deletes anything already exported.
2. **Members**: photos (each COPY is its own member, like collections) are added by a drag onto the service
   in the rail or *Add to <service>* in a photo's right-click menu; *Remove from <service>* takes them out.
3. **Four states per member**, the Lightroom vocabulary:
   - **New** — never published;
   - **Modified** — published, but its edits or the service's export settings changed since;
   - **Published** — what is at the destination is its current version;
   - **Removed** — taken out of the service, its exported copy still at the destination (shown until the next
     Publish clears it).
4. **Publish** (one button, in the service's view) exports every New and Modified member through the
   existing export worker in the background, with the usual progress and Cancel, then clears the Removed.
   One undo step is NOT offered for a publish — it acts outside the catalog (the export's rule).
5. **The service's view**: clicking it in the rail opens its members as a source, like a collection; the top
   row says the counts ("3 new · 2 modified · 40 published · 1 removed") and holds **Publish N**; each
   thumbnail of a New or Modified member carries a small corner mark (DESIGN.md's accent, no new colour).
   Removed members show in the view until cleared, dimmed.

## The two destinations

**Folder.** The exported file goes to the service's folder under the export settings' file-name template.
A re-publish **overwrites the file this service wrote for that member** (recorded path), without the
collision question — it is the service's own file. A Removed member's recorded file is deleted **only if its
size and modification time still match what the publish recorded**; otherwise it is left and the status row
says so ("Left 1 file changed since it was published"). Nothing else in the folder is ever touched.

**Immich album.** Chosen when the service is made: an existing album (the album list the rail already reads)
or a new album by name, created at the first Publish. A publish uploads through the existing upload path
(the **upload key**, `immich_upload_key`) and adds the asset to the album. A re-publish of a Modified member
uploads the new version, adds it, and **removes the old asset from the album — never deletes it** from
Immich (⛔ deleting Immich assets was declined on purpose: `immich_upload.rs`). A Removed member is removed
from the album, the asset kept. ⚠ This needs three new calls in the `immich` crate: create an album, add
assets to an album, remove assets from an album; the key needs `album.create`, `albumAsset.create` and
`albumAsset.delete`, and a refusal names the missing permission. ⚠⚠ **The DTOs are constructed from the
OpenAPI schema** (this machine cannot read the key) — the fixtures say so, and the first real publish is the
check (CLAUDE.md: a schema is not a reply).

## What "changed" means

A member is Modified when the **fingerprint** recorded at its last publish differs from now: a hash of the
copy's edits (`thumb_key::edits_fingerprint` is the model) **and** the service's export settings (so changing
the size marks every member Modified, as Lightroom does). Metadata changes (rating, keywords, title) also
count when the settings embed metadata — the builder's call is to include the export's `FileMetadata` in the
hash when `metadata` is not *Copyright only*.

## Storage

Catalog **v18** (builder's call on exact columns):
- `publish_services` — id, name UNIQUE COLLATE NOCASE, kind (`folder` | `immich`), the destination (folder
  path, or album id + name), the export settings as JSON (`ExportOptions` — a SNAPSHOT, chosen from a saved
  export preset or the defaults when made; never a live link to the preset), created.
- `publish_members` — service, the photo (keyed like the other per-copy tables), added, removed flag.
- `published` — service, the photo, fingerprint, where (file path + size + mtime, or Immich asset id), at.

⚠⚠ The per-photo tables join **`PER_FILE_TABLES`** (a move must carry them), and forget/restore carries them
as it carries collections. A deleted service drops its rows only.

## Constraints

- **CLAUDE.md applies whole**: no egui in lib modules (the states, the fingerprint, the publish plan are lib
  code); no file over 1,000 lines (`collections_panel.rs` is 779 — the rail section goes in its own module);
  hover text one sentence, no full stop; tests wait on events with a 120 s ceiling; test directories from
  `test_dir`; every fixture says captured or constructed.
- **Nothing per frame grows with a service's members** (the rail's rule): states are derived once per
  change (the catalog's `change_count`, an edit saved) and looked up per frame.
- **Immich invariants stand**: uploads ride the upload key; a staged file is never deleted on an upload
  attempt; Immich assets are never deleted. CLAUDE.md's "writes to Immich only from Send marks" becomes
  "Send marks, export's upload and publish".
- A publish of a photo whose original is an undownloaded Immich row downloads it first, as export does — or
  is skipped and counted, whichever export does today (the builder matches it).

## Non-goals

- Web services other than Immich (Flickr, SmugMug, Adobe Portfolio…).
- Anything read back from a destination (comments, likes, edits made there).
- Publishing automatically on edit — Publish is pressed, as in Lightroom.
- Smart publish collections (rule-based membership) — later, if asked.
- Undo of a publish.

## Anti-goals

- Deleting or overwriting any file the service did not write itself.
- Deleting an Immich asset.
- A publish that silently skips a member: every one is published, failed (with its reason) or counted as
  skipped, in the status row.
