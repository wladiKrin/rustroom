# M10.13d Camera-matching colour — design

**Status:** the author said "just start" (2026-11-11); every choice below is mine, marked *(my call)*.

## Intent

A RAF should open looking like the camera's own JPEG of it — its film simulation, Fuji's tones and colours —
without the user installing anything. Rustroom ships no profiles (Adobe's `.dcp` files are not ours to
redistribute, `docs/JOURNAL.md` C4). The legal route chosen *(my call)*: **learn each film simulation's look from
the user's own photos**. Every RAF carries the camera's full-size JPEG of itself, rendered with the film
simulation it was shot with; Rustroom fits a table that maps its own render onto that JPEG.

## The spike (2026-11-11, `fit_spike`, not kept)

18 X-T4 RAFs of one shoot, all Pro Neg. Std at default JPEG settings. A 17³ lattice fitted on 13 by least squares
with a Laplacian smoothness penalty (CG, ~1 s) took the 5 held-out photos from **mean ΔE 11.4 to 4.5**
(per photo 8.9–13.8 → 3.3–7.1). λ 0.01–1 changed little. ⚠ One shoot, one simulation: how a look
**generalises across scenes** is unmeasured — the author's library decides it.

## Goals

- **Learning** — a background pass over the catalog's RAFs *(my call: Fuji only — the makernote that names the film
  simulation is Fuji's; other makes keep "Camera")*: grouped by **body and film simulation**; only photos shot at
  **default JPEG tone settings** (DR100, highlight and shadow tone 0, colour 0) take part, since those shift the
  JPEG; a group with **at least 8** such photos is fitted from **up to 40** of them, spread across the group; a
  group is re-learned when it has grown by half since its last fit. Off the UI thread; the status row says
  "Learning the camera's colour — Classic Chrome (X-T4)".
- **The result** — a `.cube` (17³, sRGB display) in the looks folder, `camera/<Film> (<Body>).cube`, and a
  manifest beside it (key → file, photos used, date). Plain files: visible, removable *(my call)*.
- **Applying** — a third rendering, **Camera matching**: Rustroom's camera rendering, then the look learned for
  *this photo's* film simulation, then the user's own look. A photo with no learned look (another make, a
  simulation not yet learned) renders exactly as **Camera**. It is the default rendering for a photo with no
  edits *(my call)*; photos already edited keep theirs. Copy/paste and sync carry "Camera matching", and each
  photo resolves its own simulation.
- **Profile block** — the rendering control offers None · Camera · Camera matching; with Camera matching on, a
  quiet line names what it applies ("Pro Neg. Std (X-T4)") or says nothing is learned for this film yet.
- **Settings → Behaviour** — "Learn the camera's colour from my photos" (on) and a **Relearn** button.

## Non-goals

- Other makes (no film-simulation tag to group by); per-photo JPEG settings (DR, tones, colour chrome) as inputs.
- Shipping any profile or table; DCP `LookTable` (M10.13b's half — not needed by this route).
- An independent colour oracle (M10.13a, parked).

## How

- **lib** (no egui): `camera_look::{makernote, fit, store, learn}` — the Fuji makernote (film simulation and the
  tone settings, from the RAF's embedded JPEG's EXIF), samples (central 80 %, flat areas, both at the same size),
  the regularised lattice fit and its `.cube` text, the manifest, and the pass's plan (groups, picks, staleness).
- **pipeline**: `Rendering::CameraMatch`; `TailRendering.camera_look`; a `camera_look` stage after
  `display_rendering` and before `creative_look` (a `CreativeLookStage` in sRGB display at strength 100 under its
  own name) with its `REPLACEABLE` GPU row and `every_tail_stage` field.
- **app**: the photo's camera look resolved where the camera profile is (the decode thread, export's decode, the
  thumbnail render) and carried the same way; the pass pumped like the location pass.
