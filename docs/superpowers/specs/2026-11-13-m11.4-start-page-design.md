# M11.4 (first half) — the start page, the welcome, and reopening where you left off

**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Branch:** `m11.4-start-page` · **Mockups:** <https://claude.ai/artifact/Hnj6DR4oeAh21SR1KGYjge>
(screens 1–3 as revised and approved by the author, 2026-11-13)

## Why

Every launch shows "No photos yet" with two buttons in an almost empty middle, even over a catalog of
2,914 photos: the Library always starts with nothing open (`bootstrap.rs` builds `library: None`). The
author wants:

- a launch to put you back where you were, by default;
- a start page that fills the middle with somewhere to go when it cannot;
- a first-launch welcome that explains what's what, until it is ticked away.

The second item of M11.4, the empty Library's text, is folded in here: the start page replaces the
empty Library.

## What the author decided

1. **Reopen where you left off, on by default.** At launch Rustroom opens what the Library last showed:
   any `LibrarySource`, whether a folder, All Photos, a collection or smart collection, a keyword, dates,
   a person, an Immich person, a publish service, or an Immich album or month.
2. **Back to the Library with the photo selected**, never Develop. That holds even when you quit while
   editing in Develop: the photo that was open, or the Library's active photo, is the selected one, and
   it is scrolled into view.
3. **The start page instead, whenever reopening does not work**: the folder is gone, the collection,
   keyword, smart collection, person or publish service was deleted, Immich is switched off or not
   configured, or Immich's reply is an error. It also appears whenever the setting is off, and on a
   first launch, when nothing has been opened yet.
4. **A setting to turn reopening off:** Settings → Behaviour, "Reopen where you left off", at the top
   of the tab.
5. **The welcome** (mockup screen 1) sits on the start page until its **"Don't show this again"** is
   ticked:
   - the heading "Welcome to Rustroom";
   - one sentence under it, in the author's words: *"Your files are never changed, edits are kept
     separately beside them."*;
   - the two buttons, Open a folder… (primary) and Import from a card…;
   - a card titled "What's where" holding two small numbered maps of the window side by side,
     **Library** and **Develop**, each with its key underneath:
     - Library: ① Library · Develop (browse, or edit one photo), ② Rail (where photos come from),
       ③ Search and Filter (narrow the grid), ④ Status row (counts, sort and jobs).
     - Develop: ⑤ Panel (the edits, in sections), ⑥ Tools (crop, masks, retouch and more),
       ⑦ Filmstrip (the other photos you have open), ⑧ Export (save the finished picture).
     - ① is the app switch in both maps; the numbering runs on across the two, so no number means two
       things.
   - "Don't show this again" as the card's last row.

   ⚠ There is **no drop hint** ("You can also drop a folder…"), by the author's call: dropping is
   intuitive. The welcome's own sentence is the only place that says files are never changed.
6. **The start page once the welcome is gone** (mockup screen 2):
   - the heading "No photos open" and the sentence "Pick up where you were, or open something new";
   - the same two buttons;
   - a **Continue** card (Caption sub-label above it) listing All Photos with its count, then up to
     five recent folders, newest first. Each folder row carries its name, its parent path in Caption
     `text3`, its photo count in the count column (§7.6), and the hover "Opens 2026-11-08". *(My call: the
     mockup's "last opened yesterday" is dropped, because `recent_folders` keeps no times.)*
     A recent folder that no longer exists is left out. One click opens the row.
   - No drop hint here either.
7. **"Show the welcome on the start page"** (my addition, accepted with the mockups) is the second new
   row in Settings → Behaviour. It is the same flag as the card's box, inverted, so a dismissed welcome
   can come back.

**When both apply** (the welcome is still on and there is something to continue), the welcome card sits
**beneath** the Continue card, by the author's call (2026-11-13). With nothing to continue (an empty
catalog and no recent folder that still exists) there is no Continue card, only the welcome, which is
mockup screen 1. The page scrolls vertically when the two together are taller than the window (the
800 × 600 minimum). The heading follows the welcome: "Welcome to Rustroom" with its sentence while the
welcome shows, and "No photos open" with its sentence once it is gone.

## How it fits the code (decided here, open to the plan)

- **Logic in the lib, widgets in the bin** (CLAUDE.md's placement rule). A new lib module,
  `rustroom_app::start`, holds no egui and owns:
  - `LastPlace`: a serialisable mirror of `LibrarySource` plus the selected `(path, copy)`;
  - the reopen decision: given a `LastPlace`, the `RailData` and whether Immich is connected, either
    a source to open or "show the start page";
  - the Continue rows, built from `recent_folders`, the filesystem and `RailData`'s counts.

  A new bin module, `start_page.rs`, draws the page and the two maps; `library::empty_library` and
  `EMPTY_LIBRARY_TEXT` go.
- **`Settings`** gains `reopen_last: bool` (default **true**), `show_welcome: bool` (default **true**) and
  `last_place: Option<LastPlace>`. ⚠ Settings carries a container-level `#[serde(default)]`, so both
  booleans must default to `true` in `Settings::default()`; that is the `auto_advance` trap, and each
  gets a test pinning it. A settings file written before this change must load with reopening on and
  the welcome showing. ⚠ The author's own settings file therefore shows the welcome once after upgrade,
  which is intended.
- **`LibrarySource` stays in the bin**; the conversion to and from `LastPlace` lives beside it.
  `LastPlace` carries ids and names exactly as `LibrarySource` does, and a date set as its `Period`
  triples.
- **When it is written:** the place mirrors what the Library shows, and that includes nothing. A pump
  late in each frame derives the place from `library` and saves when it differs from the stored one.
  A Library that closes, by forgetting a folder, deleting a collection and the like (about ten sites
  set `library = None`), therefore stores "nothing open", and the next launch shows the start page
  rather than undoing the delete. *(My call, from the builder check: one derived writer, not ten.)*
  The pump starts only after the reopen has been attempted, so the first frame's `None` never
  overwrites the place. The selection is written with it on close. ⚠ Close is handled only on macOS today (`shell/mod.rs` calls
  `persist_current_session` and exits). Saving the place on close must run on **every** platform, and
  the `process::exit` workaround stays macOS-only.
- **When it is read:** once, on the first frame, when a context exists to open with. Validity is
  checked before any opener runs:
  - a folder: it exists on disk, **or** the catalog still lists it (an unplugged drive). Either way it
    reopens through the rail's opener, `open_catalog_folder`, which falls back to the last-seen rows,
    exactly as a click on the rail would. A reopen does not reorder `recent_folders`. *(My call.)*
  - a catalog id (collection, smart collection, keyword, person, publish service): it is still in
    `RailData`;
  - dates: always valid, though they may be empty;
  - an Immich source: `immich_source::connection` is `Some`.

  An Immich reply that comes back as an error for a **reopen** request clears the Library back to the
  start page, so the status line says why. The status line already says "Could not open …".
- **Selecting the photo:** after the source opens (for Immich, when its scan lands), the entry with the
  stored path and copy becomes the single selection and the active photo, and the grid scrolls to it.
  A photo no longer in the source leaves the default selection.

## Non-goals

- No Develop restore (decided).
- Restoring the filter, search text, sort, zoom or panel layout. Those already persist through their own
  settings or not at all, and nothing changes here.
- Changing the rail or the Library's other empty states: an empty folder, or a filter that hides
  everything, keep their own sentences (`why_empty`).
- Reopening several sources or windows.

## Done when

- A launch with reopening on puts the Library back on the last source with the last photo selected,
  for a folder, All Photos, a collection and an Immich album. A test covers each kind of source, and the
  author confirms by use.
- A deleted folder or collection, or Immich switched off, lands on the start page. A test covers each.
- The start page matches mockups 1 and 2; the two Settings rows match mockup 3; every new control has a
  one-sentence hover with no full stop; and the hover sweep and the "every menu string is renderable"
  sweep both pass.
- The design rules hold: four text sizes and Display only for the heading; the Continue counts sit in
  the rail's count column; the maps use only theme tokens; and no literal font size or `Color32`
  appears outside `theme.rs`.
- The gate is green: fmt, clippy (own target dir), `cargo test --workspace`, and the goldens untouched.

## Left to the builder

- How the two maps are painted (egui shapes from theme tokens, sized to fit the card at the column's
  width). They must read like the mockup, not match it to the pixel.
- The exact hover sentences for the new Settings rows and the Continue rows, within §7.9's rule.
- Where the reopen request sits in the first-frame flow, provided it happens once and never on a ghost
  frame.
